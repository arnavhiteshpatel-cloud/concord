Summary

No clinical validation. No partnerships. No regulatory clearance. Not a medical device.

NeuroMetric is pre-incorporation research software. It has not been used with human participants, has no institutional, clinical or industry partners, has not been reviewed by any regulator, and makes no diagnostic or treatment claims.

Open issues we are working through

  • Overlap with academic instruments. Academic groups are developing digital-function assessments for BCIs and assistive technology, including cross-device work. NeuroMetric aims to complement and, where licensable, implement consensus instruments rather than compete with them.
  • Licences. Several established questionnaires require licences before their items can be used.
  • Intellectual property. The founder’s university IP position is being clarified before incorporation or any open-source release.
  • Platform access. Some platforms restrict or forbid third-party usage logging; device-maker data exports may be the more appropriate route for BCI usage data.
