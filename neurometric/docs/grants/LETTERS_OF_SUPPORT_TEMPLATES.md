# Letters of support: templates

> **TEMPLATES ONLY. No one has written, agreed to or been asked for any of these letters.**
> Prepared 2026-11-27. Each template is a starting point **for the signer to rewrite in their own
> words**. The signer decides what, if anything, they are willing to commit to; every bracketed
> commitment is optional and must be deleted if it is not true. NeuroMetric must not draft final
> wording for a signer, insert commitments the signer has not offered, or use a letter beyond the
> application it was written for.
>
> How to use:
> 1. Send the signer the one-page Specific Aims (`NIH_STTR_PHASE1.md` §1), the relevant part of
>    the approach, the honest-status paragraph below, and the matching template.
> 2. Ask them to write on their own letterhead, signed and dated, addressed as their institution
>    prefers (NIH letters are usually addressed to the PD/PI or the review committee [PK]).
> 3. Keep each letter factual: what the signer will do, with what resources, over what period.
>    Reviewers discount generic praise [PK].
> 4. NIH places letters in the "Letters of Support" attachment; check the current page limits
>    and format in the SF424 instructions [PK].

**Honest-status paragraph to send with every request (do not delete):**
> NeuroMetric is pre-incorporation research software built by a UIUC undergraduate. It has no
> human data, no clinical validation, no customers and no partners yet. Its preliminary work is
> computational (simulated virtual users and simulated input degradation) plus an analysis of one
> public macaque cursor dataset. The tasks are original and are not the Shirley Ryan AbilityLab
> Digital Assessment Interface or any licensed instrument. A letter of support from you would
> not imply endorsement of the software unless you say so.

## 1. Faculty PD/PI (partner research institution)

Required elements for NIH STTR [V rule in `docs/research/funding.md` §3.1; wording PK]: the PD/PI's
formal appointment with or commitment to the applicant small business (this need not involve
salary), effort ≥ 10%, and the institution's role.

> [Letterhead of `[PARTNER RI]`, department]
>
> [Date]
>
> To: [NIH review committee / NeuroMetric, Inc.]
>
> Re: STTR Phase I application "Development and initial validation of an input-agnostic digital
> functional outcome assessment system for brain-computer interfaces and assistive technologies"
>
> I am [title, department, institution]. My research concerns [signer's own description].
>
> **Role.** I agree to serve as [contact PD/PI | PD/PI | co-investigator] on this application.
> [Under the NIH STTR program I will have a formal commitment to NeuroMetric, Inc. as described
> in [document/agreement], which does not involve [salary | equity] from the company.] I will
> commit [X]% effort ([Y] calendar months per year), charged to the subaward to [institution].
>
> **What I will do.** [Signer to choose and edit, for example: serve as IRB Principal
> Investigator for the human-subjects studies; own the statistical analysis plans; hold the
> participant data at [institution]; supervise the research coordinator; oversee the conflict-of-
> interest management plan for the student founder.]
>
> **Resources.** [Signer to list only what is actually available: laboratory space, testing room,
> access to [patient population/clinic] through [route], equipment.]
>
> **Independence.** [Optional, if true: The company will have no veto over publication, and
> confirmatory analyses will be performed at [institution] by people other than the founder.]
>
> [Signature, name, title, contact]

What the PD/PI should **not** be asked to state: that the software is valid or effective, that a
patient population is guaranteed, recruitment numbers they have not checked, or any equity or
payment arrangement not already agreed and disclosed.

## 2. Clinical site (e.g. a hospital PM&R, neurology, ALS or SCI clinic)

> [Letterhead of `[CLINICAL SITE]`]
>
> [Date]
>
> Re: [same project title]
>
> I am [title, service, institution]. Our [clinic/programme] sees [signer's own description of the
> population, only if they are comfortable stating it].
>
> **Support.** [Signer chooses, for example: We support the aims of this project. | We are willing
> to discuss serving as a recruitment site for Aims 2 and 3, subject to our institution's research
> approval process and an IRB reliance agreement. | Members of our team are willing to advise on
> eligibility criteria and safety procedures for people with [ALS/SCI/stroke].]
>
> **Conditions.** [Signer to state any, for example: participation requires approval through
> [institution's research office]; recruitment would be by [approved method]; we cannot guarantee
> enrolment numbers.]
>
> [Signature, name, title]

Reminder: Carle recruitment requires the Carle Research Services request and a documented
reviewing IRB / reliance agreement [V route, `CLINICAL_VALIDATION_PLAN.md` §2.10]. A letter
does not replace these.

## 3. Assistive-technology clinic or AT service provider (e.g. AT/OT/SLP service, DRES-type
service, AAC clinic)

> [Letterhead]
>
> [Date]
>
> Re: [same project title]
>
> I am [title] at [organisation], which provides [signer's description: AT evaluation, AAC
> services, computer-access training ...].
>
> **Relevance.** [Signer's own view, if they hold one, of the need for outcome measures that work
> across access methods (eye gaze, switch, head pointer, BCI). Do not supply this text for them.]
>
> **What we can offer.** [Signer chooses, only if true: advice on task accessibility and access
> settings; review of the battery by our clinicians; sharing information about the study with
> clients who may be interested (participation is voluntary and through the study team); a
> usability advisory role for people with lived experience, outside the research protocol.]
>
> **What we are not committing to.** [For example: we are not a research site; we will not share
> client records; no fee or payment is involved | payment terms are ...]
>
> [Signature, name, title]

Note: a lived-experience advisory role is not research participation and needs no IRB, but
anything that collects data from these people does (`docs/research/collaborators.md` #6).

## 4. Sponsor or device/AT manufacturer

Use only after a real conversation in which the company has said what it would do. Company
letters often need legal review on their side; allow time.

> [Company letterhead]
>
> [Date]
>
> Re: [same project title]
>
> [Company] develops [signer's description of their product: non-invasive BCI, AAC device, eye
> tracker, switch interface, home rehabilitation device ...].
>
> **Interest.** [Signer chooses, only if true: We have an interest in neutral, input-agnostic
> measures of digital function for our users. | We would consider evaluating NeuroMetric in
> [a pilot / an internal study] after Phase I, subject to a separate agreement.]
>
> **Possible contribution.** [Only if offered: loan of [N] devices for bench timing; technical
> documentation for [export format]; a data-use agreement for aggregate device-usage exports
> (Tier 2) in a later phase; advisory input on what evidence our regulatory or reimbursement
> teams need.]
>
> **Limits.** [For example: This letter is not a purchase commitment, a commitment to provide
> data, or an endorsement. Any collaboration would be under a written agreement.]
>
> [Signature, name, title]

Conflict check before requesting: a sponsor that is also a potential customer or competitor
(e.g. a company with its own in-house outcome tools, or a collaborator on the SRAlab DAI study)
should be disclosed in the COI review; its letter must not shape the analysis plan.

## 5. Request e-mail (template)

> Subject: Request for a letter of support (NIH STTR, due [date]) – only if you are comfortable
>
> Dear [Name],
>
> [One sentence on how you know them or why you are writing.] We are preparing an NIH STTR Phase I
> application, due [date], to test the reliability and validity of an input-agnostic battery of
> digital tasks for people who use BCIs and assistive technology. I have attached the one-page
> aims and a short status note: the project has no human data yet and no partners have committed.
>
> Would you be willing to write a short letter describing [the specific thing discussed]? I have
> attached a template only as a starting point; please write only what you are comfortable
> committing to, and feel free to decline. If a letter would be easier after a conversation, I
> would be glad to set one up.
>
> I would need the signed letter by [date, at least 2 weeks before submission].
>
> Thank you,
> [Founder name] – NeuroMetric (pre-incorporation), [UIUC affiliation as student]
