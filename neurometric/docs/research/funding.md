## 1. Bottom line

1. **SBIR/STTR are authorized again.** Authority lapsed on 1 Oct 2025. It was restored by Public
   Law 119-83 (S. 3971, Small Business Innovation and Economic Security Act), signed 13 Apr 2026,
   and runs through **30 Sep 2031** [V](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ83/html/PLAW-119publ83.htm).
   During the lapse NIH expired every SBIR/STTR NOFO (NOT-OD-26-006, 17 Nov 2025)
   [V](https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-26-006.html).
   New NIH omnibus NOFOs were posted on 28 May 2026 and opened on 5 Aug 2026
   [V](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/359671).
2. **Best first federal route: an NIH STTR Phase I under PA-27-102 with a UIUC or Carle faculty
   PI.** The remaining standard due dates under this NOFO are **5 Jan 2027** and **5 Apr 2027**. The
   NOFO expires 6 Apr 2027 [V](https://files.simpler.grants.gov/opportunities/ee37f2de-b959-4b45-86a9-43d06c944f24/attachments/5295c0e4-bffd-4a53-911e-4cfdd9567e71/PA-27-102-Revised-Full-Announcement.html).
   Budget guidelines: **NINDS** up to $700k (Phase I) and $3.0M (Phase II); **NICHD** (home of
   NCMRR, rehabilitation) up to $400k and $2.5M; **NIDCD** up to $400k and $3.0M; **NIBIB** follows the
   SBA guideline [V] (same NOFO table).
   - [INF] 5 Jan 2027 is not realistic: the company does not exist yet and registrations take six
     weeks or more [V](https://www.grants.nih.gov/news-events/nih-extramural-nexus-news/2026/04/its-official-nihs-small-business-program-is-back).
   - [INF] Target **5 Apr 2027** if NeuroMetric incorporates by about mid-January. Otherwise target
     **5 Sep 2027** under the reissued NOFO. Its number and date are not yet published [UNR].
3. **Campus non-dilutive money and prizes**:
   - Cozad New Venture Challenge 2027: registration opened Fall 2026 [V](https://landuyt.illinois.edu/programs/cozad/faq).
     The 2026 grand prize was $100k, paid as a SAFE.
   - Illinois I-Corps: 2027 application deadline 5 Feb, with a route to the NSF I-Corps National Teams
     $50k grant [V](https://icorps.illinois.edu/programs/lean-launchpad).
   - IP Clinic: free patent and trademark drafting for Cozad and iVenture startups.
4. **Two "prior-cycle" programs fit NeuroMetric well**. Watch for their FY27 versions:
   - **DoD ALSRP Clinical Outcomes and Biomarkers Award (COBA).** Up to $1.0M. It explicitly invites
     performance outcomes and digital health measures. FY26 deadlines were a 24 Jun 2026
     pre-application and a 30 Sep 2026 full application
     [V](https://cdmrp.health.mil/funding/pa/HT942526ALSRPCOBA_GG.pdf).
   - **NIDILRR SBIR Phase I.** $95-100k. The FY27 forecast lists a due date of **16 Dec 2026**
     [V](https://simpler.grants.gov/opportunity/363966). NeuroMetric cannot make this date unincorporated;
     plan for the next cycle.
5. **Neurotech VCs do not fund pre-seed outcome-software companies.** Synchron, Axoft and Kandu
   (Neurolutions) raised from device-stage funds. Treat those funds as later-stage targets and the
   device companies as customers.
   - [INF] Near-term equity is most realistic from Illinois Ventures pre-seed ($100-300k)
     [V](https://illinoisventures.com/pre-seed-fund/), Illini Angels and Cozad SAFE prizes.

## 3. SBIR vs STTR rules, and how a student founder can take part

### 3.1 Rules that matter (NIH vs NSF)

| Rule | NIH SBIR (PA-27-100) | NIH STTR (PA-27-102) | NSF SBIR/STTR (26-510) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who receives the award | The small business | The small business ("Regardless of the PD/PI employment, the award is always made to the small business") | The small business |
| PI employment | PD/PI "should be primarily employed by the small business" [V]; >50% at award [PK] | PD/PI "may be employed with the SBC or the single, 'partnering' non-profit research institution as long as s/he has a formal appointment with or commitment to the applicant SBC… Such a relationship does not necessarily involve a salary." Each PD/PI commits **at least 10% effort** [V]. | PI must be **at least 51% employed by the small business** at award and for the whole award. Outside employment over 19.6 hours a week conflicts. This applies to **both SBIR and STTR**. An STTR co-PI must come from the partner research institution [V]. |
| Work split | Phase I: about 67% by the company. Phase II: about 50% [V]. | At least 40% by the company and at least 30% by one partnering nonprofit research institution [V]. | Research-institution minimum not stated in the solicitation [V]; statutory 30% [PK]. |
| Ownership | More than 50% owned by US citizens or permanent residents, or by multiple VC/PE firms (NIH allows VC-majority firms up to its set-aside limit) [V]. | Same [V]. | Similar [PK]. |
| IP between company and university | Normal university rules. | An allocation-of-rights (IP) agreement between the company and the research institution is required before award (SBA Policy Directive) [PK, not re-verified]. | NSF takes no equity [V]. The STTR IP agreement is required [PK]. |

**Key difference [V].** At NIH, a UIUC, Carle or SRAlab faculty member can be the STTR PI while
staying employed at their institution. At NSF, the PI must be the company's employee (at least 51%)
even for STTR. That makes NSF STTR hard for a part-time student founder or a full-time faculty
member.

### 3.2 Practical participation model for NeuroMetric [INF unless labelled]

1. **Incorporate first.** No federal award, NIH eRA Commons organization account, SAM/UEI or
   Grants.gov registration is possible without a legal entity. [V] NIH warns registrations take
   six weeks or more.
   Also needed: an EIN, SAM.gov UEI, Grants.gov, eRA Commons, and the SBA company registry
   [PK, standard requirements].
2. **NIH STTR with a faculty PI** (for example Yuan Yang, UIUC Bioengineering and Carle; see
   `collaborators.md`).
   - The faculty PI holds a formal, possibly unpaid, commitment letter to NeuroMetric and puts in at
     least 10% effort [V].
   - UIUC or Carle receives a subaward for at least 30% of the work [V].
   - The founder can be key personnel, or a multiple PI if NIH's multi-PI rules are met. Every
     PD/PI must meet the 10% effort and formal-commitment test [V].
3. **SBIR with the founder as PI is a weak first option.** The PI should be primarily employed by
   the company [V]. A full-time undergraduate would be a hard sell to reviewers [INF]. Whether a
   full-time student can be primarily employed by the company is [UNR]; ask NIH SEED or the program
   officer.
4. **Student employment.** Paying the founder from a grant requires company payroll. [PK]
   International founders need to check CPT/OPT work authorization before taking a salary or
   signing as a company officer. The Cozad FAQ also warns that company-registration rules differ
   for international students and that visa status should be protected
   [V](https://landuyt.illinois.edu/programs/cozad/faq).
5. **University IP (UIUC General Rules, Article III).**
   - The University claims IP made with significant University resources, in employment, or under
     sponsored projects. Students who meet none of those conditions generally own their work.
     Exceptions are decided by OTM and the VP for Research. The rules were last amended May 2023
     [AE](https://www.bot.uillinois.edu/userfiles/Servers/Server_694865/file/General-Rules.pdf)
     (from a search summary; text not fetched).
   - Action: write down that NeuroMetric's code was built outside any RA job or lab resources, and
     get OTM to confirm ownership before Cozad or IP Clinic filings.
   - The Cozad FAQ says the University and the Landuyt Center take **no IP** from participation or
     prizes [V].
6. **Faculty conflict of interest.** If a faculty PI or advisor takes equity, UIUC
   conflict-of-commitment and conflict-of-interest disclosure applies [PK].
