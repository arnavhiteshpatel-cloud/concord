# Research conventions (all NeuroMetric research files)

Evidence labels (inherited from the founder playbook, keep them):
- [V] verified this session against a primary source (URL given, accessed date given)
- [CC] company claim (press release, company website)
- [RF] peer-reviewed research finding
- [AE] analyst / third-party estimate
- [INF] inference by NeuroMetric (reasoning, not a source)
- [HYP] hypothesis to be tested (prices, willingness to pay, market sizes)
- [PK] prior knowledge not re-verified this session
- [UNR] unresolved: could not be verified

Rules:
1. Every factual claim carries a label and, for V/CC/RF/AE, a URL.
2. Never upgrade a label without a source. If a playbook claim could not be confirmed, keep its
   original label and say "not re-verified".
3. Record the access date (today is 2026-11-27) for web sources.
4. Never invent people, contacts, prices, partnerships, deadlines or study results.
5. Name only public contact routes (institutional pages, published general inboxes, registry
   contacts). Do not collect personal phone numbers or private emails.
6. Dates and deadlines: state the source and say if it is from a prior cycle.
