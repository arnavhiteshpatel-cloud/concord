# NeuroMetric collaborator map

Prepared 2026-11-27. Follows `CONVENTIONS.md`. Machine-readable companion: `collaborators.csv`.

**How this file was checked.**
- Affiliations were checked against one of three sources, all accessed 2026-11-27:
  - the person's own institutional or lab page;
  - the iBCI-CC website;
  - author affiliations on their most recent PubMed records (NIH E-utilities). These carry [V]
    with the PubMed or DOI link.
- Key-publication DOIs were checked with Crossref or PubMed; they carry [RF].
- Web search ran out partway through, so some profile URLs could not be found or confirmed. Those
  carry [UNR] and say why.

**Contact rule.** Only institutional or lab pages, published general inboxes, and registry contacts
are listed. Personal emails appear only where the person's institutional page publishes them, and
no phone numbers are copied. **No one has been contacted.** Nothing in this file is a partnership or
commitment.

**Priority scale.**
- 1 = approach in the next 0-3 months, because of direct fit plus a realistic route.
- 2 = approach in months 3-12, or reach through a group such as an iBCI-CC workgroup.
- 3 = keep in view: industry-affiliated, low access, or only partly verified.

### 1. Shirley Ryan AbilityLab: DAI study team (NCT07407725) - Priority 1

**Registry record** [V](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07407725) (via the API, accessed 2026-11-27):
- **Title:** "Development of a Clinical Outcome Assessment for Assistive Technologies and
  Brain-Computer-Interfaces".
- **Sponsor:** Shirley Ryan AbilityLab. **Collaborator:** Neuralink Corp.
- **Status:** Recruiting. Started 8 Jan 2026. Target enrollment 60 (ALS, SCI, healthy controls).
- **PI and responsible party:** **Arun Jayaraman, PT, PhD**, Director of the Max Nader Center for
  Rehabilitation Technologies & Outcomes Research.
- **Central contacts:** Arun Jayaraman and **Richa Rai, PhD**. **Site contacts:** Richa Rai and
  **Sara Prokup, PT, DPT**.

> Playbook correction: the playbook said the registry lists no named PI. That is **wrong**. A PI is
> named, and two central contacts are listed.

**Outcomes in the record [V]:**
- Performance outcomes from a simulated digital activities-of-daily-living (ADL) interface: click
  accuracy, time to target, errors, cursor trajectory, words per minute, percent correct characters.
  Tasks are click, scroll, type and click-and-drag.
- NASA-TLX and SUS, both self- and clinician-reported.
- A modified COPM and the PIAD questionnaire.
- Secondary aims: validity, reliability, responsiveness, and floor/ceiling effects.

**Focus.** Rehabilitation technology and outcomes research. Jayaraman's 2026 work includes
therapy-performance prediction of stroke recovery [V].

**Key publications:**
- Registry record NCT07407725 [V].
- "Therapy Performance Predicts Functional Recovery After Stroke", *Neurorehabil Neural Repair*
  2026, [doi:10.1177/15459683261501509](https://doi.org/10.1177/15459683261501509) [RF].
- Skin-integrated wireless sensor networks, *PNAS* 2021,
  [doi:10.1073/pnas.2104925118](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2104925118) [RF].

**Why relevant [INF].** This is the closest existing effort to NeuroMetric's thesis: a standardized,
input-agnostic index of digital independence for AT and BCIs. Being in Chicago and close to UIUC and
Carle helps.

**What NeuroMetric can contribute [INF].** An input-agnostic event-logging SDK and scoring pipeline
that could:
- carry the same performance metrics into home and real-world use; and
- add psychometric tooling (reliability, minimal detectable change, responsiveness).

**Proposed project [HYP].** A "DAI-to-real-world" bridging sub-study. Consenting participants
would run the NeuroMetric logger at home for 2-4 weeks. Agreement would then be tested between
in-clinic DAI performance outcomes and real-world digital-activity metrics.
- Needs SRAlab's agreement, an IRB amendment, and attention to Neuralink's role as collaborator,
  including data rights and confidentiality.
- A fallback is to offer NeuroMetric as an analysis or logging tool under the team's own protocol.

**Public contact route.**
- Central contacts listed on the ClinicalTrials.gov record above.
- [SRAlab researcher profile](https://www.sralab.org/researchers/arun-jayaraman-pt-phd) [V].
