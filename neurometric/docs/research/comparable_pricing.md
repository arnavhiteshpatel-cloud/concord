# Comparable pricing for NeuroMetric pricing hypotheses

Prepared 2026-11-27. Labels per `CONVENTIONS.md`. All URLs accessed 2026-11-27. The founder price points are
**hypotheses [HYP]**, not facts: pilots $25-75K; $100-400K per study-year; $100-300K/yr maker contracts.

**Headline** [INF]: public pricing in this market is scarce. eCOA/ePRO/DCT vendors (Medidata, Medable,
Signant, Clario, YPrime, Suvoda, Castor paid tiers, Koneksa) do **not** publish per-study or per-participant
prices. The few hard public numbers are (a) OpenClinica's per-study EDC list prices, (b) Castor's free academic
tier, (c) Compass AT-assessment licences, and (d) professional-services hourly rates. Everything else is
analyst/vendor estimates. No public price was found for any BCI-specific outcome service (no such commercial
offer was found).

## 5. Testing the founder hypotheses [INF unless labelled]

**H1: Pilots $25-75K [HYP].** *Plausible.* A software-only small academic EDC study-year is ~$10-50K [AE];
OpenClinica Core/Advanced list at $36-45K per study-year [V]. A NeuroMetric pilot that bundles software,
task battery setup, home telemetry and a short evidence report can credibly sit at $25-75K, but the lower bound
competes with free academic tools (Castor free tier [CC], REDCap [PK], open BCI software [V]) and $179 AT
assessment software [V]. Implication: price pilots on the evidence deliverable (validation report, cross-device
comparison) rather than software seats; expect academic pilots near the low end and grant-funded.

**H2: $100-400K per study-year [HYP].** *Partly supported, upper end at risk.* The range overlaps EDC Phase II-III
year-1 costs ($50-500K+) [AE], but those studies have 5-50+ sites and hundreds of participants. iBCI EFS
studies register 2-15 participants [V CT.gov], so $100-400K implies roughly $10-40K per participant-year,
which is high against eCOA norms unless NeuroMetric delivers substantial services (home task administration,
psychometric analysis, regulatory-grade evidence package). Pivotal iBCI studies (likely tens of participants,
several sites [INF]) are the better fit for $200-400K. Recommend testing $100-150K for EFS and $250-400K for
pivotal in discovery.

**H3: $100-300K/yr maker contracts [HYP].** *Comparable exists at the top end, but buyer count is the
constraint.* A $188K median annual contract for a full clinical software portfolio (Veeva, Vendr data) [AE]
shows that device/biotech companies pay this order of magnitude for platform contracts. But (a) only ~3 US iBCI
sponsors are active and ~5-8 plausible by 2028 (`competitors.md` S5), (b) the largest (Neuralink) builds in-house,
and (c) AT makers' clinic-facing assessment software is priced at hundreds of dollars ($179 Compass [V]) and
AAC analytics are subscription-priced and unpublished [V]. A $100-300K/yr contract is plausible only for
(i) non-dominant iBCI sponsors in pivotal preparation and (ii) AT/AAC makers buying payer-evidence programs,
not clinic tools. Test with AT makers framed around coverage evidence, not assessment software.

**Overall** [INF]: the comparables neither confirm nor refute the price points; they set a floor
(free academic EDC; $179 AT assessment) and a ceiling (pharma-scale eCOA/EDC study-years of $150-500K+). The
decisive unknown is willingness to pay for *independence and payer-grade packaging*, which only discovery
conversations can resolve. Suggested falsification test: if ≥3 of the first 8 qualified sponsor/maker
conversations will not consider ≥$50K for a 6-month evidence pilot, revise H1-H3 downward or re-scope to
grant-funded academic work.
