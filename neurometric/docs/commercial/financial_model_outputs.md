## 5. How to read and challenge this model

- Commercial revenue excludes grants. Grants appear below operating income; STTR subawards to the research partner (>=30%) are deducted.
- Delivery labour sits in COGS by the hour; the headcount plan excludes delivery staff. The 'Delivery FTE equivalent' row shows how many people delivery implies.
- New customers pay for a pilot in their first year; retained customers pay contract value. ARR run-rate assumes pilots convert at the segment retention rate.
- Taxes, depreciation, interest, founder equity dilution and FX are ignored.
- The decisive unknowns are willingness to pay (pricing test in PRICING_HYPOTHESES.md), the number of sponsors that buy third-party evidence at all, and STTR award timing.
