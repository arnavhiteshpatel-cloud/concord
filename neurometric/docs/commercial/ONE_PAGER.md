# NeuroMetric - one page

*Draft 2026-11-27. For conversations the founder chooses to have; not distributed.*

**What it is.** NeuroMetric is research software for **neutral, input-agnostic measurement of functional
outcomes** for brain-computer interfaces (BCIs), assistive technology (AT) and home neuromodulation. It records
how people actually use their access method at home (use time, independent vs assisted sessions, task completion,
communication rate), runs short standardized digital tasks, captures care-partner burden, and packages the data
with an audit trail for research, regulatory and payer audiences. It never controls a device or stimulation.
It is not a medical device and makes no diagnostic or treatment claims.

**Why now.**
- The field says the measures are missing: the iBCI Collaborative Community's consensus (1 Oct 2026) states that a
  single existing outcome measure is unlikely to be sufficient and calls for real-world data and a measurement
  chain from device performance to function to care burden [V].
- FDA's implanted-BCI guidance asks for home-use evidence and caregiver metrics; no BCI outcome assessment is
  qualified under FDA's MDDT program [V].
- Payers say the evidence is missing: a major commercial payer policy (Anthem DME.00052) lists real-world use and
  daily-living independence as unproven for BCI rehabilitation devices [V].

**Who it is for (hypotheses being tested).** Non-invasive BCI and AAC makers, home-neuromodulation and rehab-device
makers, academic BCI/rehab programs, and implantable-BCI sponsors preparing pivotal studies.

**How it is different (intended).** Works across inputs (BCI, eye-gaze, switch, EMG, voice); focuses on the home,
where sponsors and clinic instruments have least visibility; implements consensus instruments instead of a
proprietary score; links care-partner burden to observed use; designed for 21 CFR Part 11 audit expectations.

**Honest status (Nov 2026).**
- Pre-incorporation; founder is a UIUC undergraduate.
- Software: event schema, task battery, metrics, psychometrics library, degradation simulator.
- **Results are simulation-only.** No clinical validation, no human-subject data, no regulatory submissions.
- **No customers, no partners, no letters of intent.** No organisation has endorsed NeuroMetric.
- Part 11 validation package and SOC 2 not started.
