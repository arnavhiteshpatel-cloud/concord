# Risk/benefit assessment; adverse-event and unanticipated-problem reporting

**DRAFT — not submitted — requires faculty PI and IRB review**

## 1. Risks
| Risk | Likelihood [INF] | Severity [INF] | Population | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fatigue | Common | Mild, transient | All, especially ALS | Breaks; NRS stop rules; split sessions |
| Frustration or distress at low performance | Occasional | Mild | AT users | Framing ("some tasks are hard for everyone"); no scores shown against norms; stop any time |
| Eye strain or dry eye | Occasional | Mild | Eye-gaze users, Stage 2 | Breaks; glare control |
| Neck, shoulder or back discomfort | Occasional | Mild | Head pointer, joystick, prolonged sitting | Positioning; breaks; stop |
| Pressure injury risk from sitting | Rare | Moderate | Wheelchair users | Usual pressure-relief schedule; session caps |
| Autonomic dysreflexia trigger | Rare | Potentially serious | SCI T6 and above | Participant's own plan recorded; trained staff; immediate stop; plan followed; emergency services if indicated |
| Respiratory fatigue | Rare | Moderate | ALS | Usual ventilation routine; stop on request |
| Fall or transfer injury at visits | Rare | Moderate | Mobility-impaired | Staff do not transfer; accessible facilities; home-visit option |
| Device conflict at home (agent) | Rare | Mild | Stage 5 | Tested build; immediate removal |
| Confidentiality breach | Rare | Mild to moderate (health-related data) | All | DMP controls; no content capture |
| Feeling surveilled | Possible | Mild | Stage 5 | Opt-in tiers; indicator; pause |
| Undue influence through the COI | Possible | Integrity risk | All | COI plan |

## 2. Benefits
No direct benefit. Possible societal benefit: openly specified, validated measures of digital
access for people with disabilities. Compensation is not a benefit.

## 3. Risk/benefit judgement
Risks are minimal and manageable with the safeguards above. Knowledge to be gained is reasonable
relative to them [INF]. **The IRB decides.**

## 4. Definitions (UIUC OPRS)
- **Unexpected adverse event:** any adverse event "whose nature, severity, or frequency" does not
  match the risks described in the protocol documents or the expected progression of the
  participant's underlying disease [V paraphrase]
  https://oprs.research.illinois.edu/research-topics/adverse-events.
- **Related:** "It is the responsibility of the Illinois Principal Investigator to make the
  initial determination" of relatedness [V].
- **Prompt reporting:** adverse events that may represent unanticipated problems involving risks
  to participants or others are reported **within 10 business days** of learning of them, using
  the OPRS Report Form [V]. Events that are not UPs are not promptly reported but are summarized
  at continuing review [V].
- The unanticipated-problems page and its criteria exist [V title]
  (https://oprs.research.illinois.edu/research-topics/unanticipated-problems). Their content was
  not read this session, and the final protocol must quote them.

## 5. Procedure
1. **At the session:**
   - stop the session;
   - ensure safety, calling emergency services (911) when needed;
   - follow the participant's own plan (autonomic dysreflexia, ventilation);
   - notify the faculty PI the same day.
2. **Record** every adverse event (expected or not) in the AE log: date, description, severity
   (mild, moderate, severe), seriousness (death, life-threatening, hospitalization, disability,
   other important medical event [PK definitions]), relatedness (PI judgement), expectedness,
   actions and outcome.
3. **PI assessment** within 2 business days [HYP internal target]: is this an unanticipated
   problem? (unexpected + related or possibly related + places people at greater risk [PK
   standard OHRP criteria]).
4. **Report** UPs, serious or unexpected related events, noncompliance and breaches through the
   OPRS Report Form within 10 business days [V]. Faster if a partner site's IRB rules require it
   (e.g. Carle, Northwestern).
5. **Multi-site:** for any site under another IRB, report to both IRBs per the reliance agreement
   (`reliance_and_multisite.md`).
6. **Protocol deviations** are logged (study module `protocol_deviations` table) and reported per
   OPRS deviation guidance [V title: "Deviations"; content not read].
7. **Continuing review / annual check-in:** summary of all AEs and deviations.

## 6. Data and safety monitoring
- Minimal risk, so the plan proposes PI monitoring with a monthly team safety review (AE log,
  stop-rule triggers, withdrawals and their reasons, complaints).
- **Stopping rules for the study:**
  - any serious related AE pauses enrolment pending PI and IRB review;
  - more than 20% of sessions in any cohort stopped for fatigue triggers a protocol review of
    session length [HYP].
- No independent DSMB is proposed [INF]. The IRB may require otherwise.

## 7. Incidental findings
The battery is not diagnostic. If staff notice an apparent medical problem (e.g. AD symptoms), it
is handled as a safety event: stop, follow the participant's plan, and suggest they contact their
clinician. Task scores are **never** reported as clinical findings to participants or clinicians.
