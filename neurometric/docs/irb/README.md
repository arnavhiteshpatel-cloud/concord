## Who can be PI: verified
- UIUC policy **RP-19**, "Eligibility to Serve as Principal Investigator of a Research Activity
  Governed by Compliance Requirements" (last revised May 16, 2025), says that "An individual with
  an appointment in one of the following categories may not serve as a Principal Investigator",
  and lists "Undergraduate or graduate student employee". The policy's definition of a
  Compliance Protocol includes IRB oversight.
  [V] https://cam.illinois.edu/policies/rp-19/
- OPRS FAQ: people who are not eligible "must find someone willing to oversee their research as
  the responsible PI" and are listed on the Research Team Form.
  [V] https://oprs.research.illinois.edu/faqs/who-can-be-principal-investigator-pi
- **Conclusion:** the student founder **cannot** be PI. A faculty member (professor ranks with a
  significant campus presence may serve without additional approval, per RP-19 [V]) must be PI.
  The student is a research team member.
- RP-19 names student *employees*. The pages read do not address a student with no appointment
  [UNR]. That does not change the conclusion, because OPRS requires a responsible PI for anyone
  not eligible.

## Submission route (best effort, OPRS format)
- **System:** IRBOnline (https://irbonline.research.illinois.edu) [V link on the OPRS home page].
- **Consent:** the OPRS consent templates are the required starting point for the final forms.
  The drafts here supply content to paste into the OPRS "Consent Document" template
  (able-bodied) and the "Consent and Authorization Document" if HIPAA applies. OPRS also lists a
  Consent Checklist and a Witness Signature Page.
  [V] https://oprs.research.illinois.edu/research-topics/consent-templates
- **Review level:** only OPRS and the IRB decide whether a study is exempt, minimal risk or
  greater than minimal risk [V]
  https://oprs.research.illinois.edu/research-topics/does-my-study-need-irb-approval.
  We expect minimal risk [INF].
- **Reporting:** the OPRS Report Form is due "within 10 business days" of the team becoming
  aware of a reportable problem [V]
  https://oprs.research.illinois.edu/contacts/problem-information-or-event-reporting.
