# Consent document: care partners (Stage 4 diary; Stage 5; longitudinal follow-up)

**DRAFT — not submitted — requires faculty PI and IRB review**

The care partner is a research participant in their own right. They sign their own consent, and
the AT user's consent does not cover them. The care partner's taking part is independent of the
AT user's: either may decline without affecting the other, except that the care-partner diary
ends if the AT user withdraws.

**University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign: Research Consent (Care Partner)**
**Study:** Measuring computer use with assistive technology
**Principal Investigator:** *[FACULTY PI NAME]*, *[contact]*. **IRB #:** *[number]*

## Key information
- You are invited because you help *[participant]*, who is in this study. Taking part is
  voluntary and separate from their decision.
- **What you would do:**
  - a **short daily form, about 2 minutes, for 7 days** (Stage 4) or 14 days (home study), about
    the help you gave yesterday with computer or tablet use and other daily tasks;
  - a few questions about yourself (age range, relationship type, hours of help per week);
  - optionally, rating how much effort the study took.
- **Risks:** time and possible inconvenience; thinking about caregiving may be tiring or
  upsetting; a small privacy risk.
- **Benefits:** none directly.
- **Payment:** *[amount per day / per visit attended]* [HYP amounts in the plan].
- A student on the team founded a company that may sell this software (see "Financial interest").

## What we ask
- **Daily form questions** (original, not a validated questionnaire):
  - minutes helping with setting up or fixing the device;
  - minutes helping with computer or tablet tasks;
  - minutes helping with other daily activities;
  - whether the participant needed help to start or finish a digital task;
  - how burdensome yesterday felt (0–10).
- Questionnaires about caregiver burden that are owned by others (e.g. the Zarit Burden
  Interview) are used **only if** the study obtains a licence. In that case this form is updated
  and re-approved.
- If you attend sessions, you may help with setup. We note when help is given. You do not answer
  questions on the participant's behalf.

## Not collected
Your name on study data (a code is used); audio, video or photos of you; information about
other people in the household.

## Confidentiality, rights, withdrawal, contacts
- Same protections as the participant form.
- You may skip any question or day.
- If you stop, your data are kept unless you ask us to delete them.
- Rights and concerns: OPRS, irb@illinois.edu.
- Your answers are **not shared with the participant** by the study team. The participant may
  of course see the form if you fill it in together.

## If you are worried about the person you care for, or about yourself
This study is not a support service. The team will give you a list of community resources
*[IRB-approved list]* on request. If the team learns of immediate danger to someone, they will
follow the university's reporting rules *[see the OPRS Mandatory Reporting guidance; content not
read this session, UNR]*. This is explained here so that you can decide what to share.
