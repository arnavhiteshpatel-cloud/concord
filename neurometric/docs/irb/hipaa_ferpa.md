# HIPAA and FERPA considerations

**DRAFT — not submitted — requires faculty PI and IRB review**

## 1. HIPAA
- **Covered entities** under HIPAA are health plans, clearinghouses and providers who conduct
  standard electronic transactions. A **business associate** "creates, receives, maintains, or
  transmits protected health information" on behalf of a covered entity (45 CFR 160.103) [V via
  `docs/research/regulatory_landscape.md` §8.2].
- **NeuroMetric and the UIUC lab in this protocol [INF]:**
  - The study collects health-related information (self-reported diagnosis, task performance)
    **directly from participants** for research. It is not obtained from a covered entity, and
    the lab is not acting as a health-care provider billing electronically.
  - On that basis HIPAA probably does not apply to the core Stages 1–5 data. Common Rule
    confidentiality protections and university data policy still apply.
  - Whether any UIUC unit involved is part of a HIPAA "covered component" of the university
    (hybrid entity) is [UNR]. The faculty PI and OPRS must confirm.
- **HIPAA does apply when:**
  - recruiting through, or confirming diagnosis with, a covered entity such as **Carle** clinics
    or **SRAlab** (a provider) [INF]. The covered entity needs a HIPAA authorization (OPRS
    "Consent and Authorization Document" template [V exists]) or an IRB or privacy-board waiver
    of authorization, plus a data-use agreement for any limited data set;
  - Stage 6 partner studies run at a covered entity, where the partner's HIPAA rules govern.
- **Records retention:** six years when HIPAA records are involved [V OPRS record retention page].
- **State neural-data laws:** NeuroMetric logs interaction events, not neural signals. Colorado's
  definition ("generated by the measurement of" nervous-system activity) may arguably reach
  decoded outputs, which needs legal review [INF, `regulatory_landscape.md` §8.1]. This matters
  for Stage 6 BCI partners.

## 2. FERPA
- OPRS: written consent to meet FERPA requirements is "a separate requirement" from research
  consent, and "the IRB is unable to issue a waiver of consent for FERPA requirements" [V]
  https://oprs.research.illinois.edu/research-topics/research-illinois-students-and-student-data.
- **This protocol uses no student education records.** It does not use rosters, grades, DRES
  accommodation records, registrar email lists or class participation. UIUC students may respond
  to public flyers like anyone else.
- **DRES:** DRES may share flyers (if it agrees). It must **not** identify students to the team or
  share accommodation records. Any DRES-facilitated recruitment is described to the IRB, and DRES
  decides its own participation [INF].
- **Power differential:** the student founder does not recruit, consent or run sessions for
  classmates, students in courses they TA, club members or co-workers. The faculty PI does not
  recruit their own current students. Such people may still take part through general
  recruitment, with another team member running their sessions and data kept from the founder
  [INF; standard undue-influence safeguard].
- Campus mass-email approval processes: [UNR], to be confirmed with OPRS/OVCRI guidance
  ("Understanding Student Data and FERPA" is referenced by OPRS [V]).
