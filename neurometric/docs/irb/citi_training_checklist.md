# Human-subjects training checklist (CITI)

**DRAFT — not submitted — requires faculty PI and IRB review**

## Requirement (verified 2026-11-27)
- "Illinois requires the CITI 'Core IRB Training' module for all individuals engaged in human
  subject research." "All other courses are optional." [V]
  https://oprs.research.illinois.edu/training/human-subjects-research-training-citi
- Training must be complete **before** OPRS/IRB approval [V same page].
- Validity: Illinois honours a certificate for **three years** from completion. "After three
  years, training will need to be repeated." [V]
- Team members from other institutions "generally follow training requirements from their own
  institution". People without a NetID go through a reliance consultation [V].
- A separate OPRS page covers **GCP training**. Its requirement was not stated on the CITI page
  [V]. GCP is probably not required for minimal-risk non-drug, non-device studies, but may be
  required by a sponsor or by NIH policy for NIH-funded clinical trials [PK]. Confirm with OPRS.
- IRBOnline system training is also offered [V page title].
