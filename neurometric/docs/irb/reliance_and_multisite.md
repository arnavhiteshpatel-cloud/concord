# Reliance agreements and multi-site research

**DRAFT — not submitted — requires faculty PI and IRB review**

## 1. Ground rules at UIUC (verified 2026-11-27)
- "Individual investigators at Illinois may not determine if an external IRB can be used."
  Start with a **Reliance Consultation Request** to OPRS, early, ideally before any grant
  application [V] https://oprs.research.illinois.edu/reliance/single-irb-process.
- Single-IRB (sIRB) review is mandated by federal rules for "many federally-funded, multi-site"
  studies. The model "is not used for exempt research, non-U.S. sites" and some award types
  (K, T, F) [V same page].
- Non-Illinois individuals are not added to the Research Team Form. Contact OPRS instead [V]
  https://oprs.research.illinois.edu/research-topics/im-working-non-illinois-researchers-or-study-team-members.
  Partners that only distribute flyers, and do not answer study questions or take part in
  consent, are not engaged in research [V same page].

## 2. Carle (Carle Foundation Hospital / Carle Health) [V]
https://oprs.research.illinois.edu/reliance/research-carle
1. Submit the **Carle Research Services Request Form**. "This is NOT an IRB submission."
2. Carle Research Services and the Carle IRB decide whether the **Carle IRB or the Illinois IRB
   leads**.
3. Submit to the designated IRB. If it is Carle, Carle forwards materials to Illinois OPRS.
4. "Once the reviewing IRB is set, a formal reliance agreement will be documented between the
   Carle and Illinois IRBs."
5. "Research at Carle cannot begin without explicit confirmation". Activation is coordinated with
   Carle Research Services.

Contacts named on the OPRS page: researchservices@carle.com and IRB@carle.com [V].
Expected relevance: recruiting Carle patients (Stage 4 stroke/SCI), Carle clinicians as
co-investigators, the JNERL (Illinois–Carle) collaboration (Stage 6). HIPAA applies at Carle
(`hipaa_ferpa.md`).

## 3. Shirley Ryan AbilityLab / Northwestern
- "Regulatory oversight is provided by the Northwestern University IRB for Shirley Ryan Ability
  Lab (SRAlab)" under a Memorandum of Understanding. When Northwestern faculty are involved,
  "Northwestern University and SRAlab are one site" [V]
  https://irb.northwestern.edu/reliance/nu-affiliates-chicagoland-partners.html.
- Northwestern "is a participating institution of SMART IRB". A new study (or a reliance request)
  is first submitted in eIRB+ with a SMART IRB Letter of Acknowledgement or an Online Reliance
  System project ID. Questions: irbreliance@northwestern.edu [V]
  https://irb.northwestern.edu/reliance/smart-irb-irex.html.
- UIUC's SMART IRB participation was not confirmed (the participant directory loads
  dynamically) [UNR]. OPRS will know.
- **Scenarios:**
  - (a) NeuroMetric staff are not engaged. SRAlab uses NeuroMetric software under its own
    protocol and the Northwestern IRB. UIUC IRB review may not be needed for UIUC people who are
    not engaged. Ask OPRS.
  - (b) UIUC personnel are engaged, e.g. receiving identifiable data or interacting with
    participants. A reliance arrangement is set up by OPRS and Northwestern.
- Neuralink's collaborator role on NCT07407725 may impose confidentiality or data terms.
  These are a separate agreement, not an IRB matter.

## 4. Other partners (Stage 6)
Each partner's IRB is the reviewing IRB for its participants. UIUC engagement is assessed by
OPRS. Federally funded multi-site work (e.g. an NIH STTR with a UIUC faculty PI) will probably
fall under sIRB rules; consult OPRS before submitting the grant [V advice].

## 5. Checklist before any multi-site activity
- [ ] Reliance Consultation Request submitted to OPRS
- [ ] Reviewing IRB designated; reliance or authorization agreement executed
- [ ] Local context review at each site (site-specific requirements, HIPAA)
- [ ] Data-use agreement and, if needed, a subaward or contract through university offices
- [ ] COI disclosures made to each IRB
- [ ] Training reciprocity confirmed. Team members from other institutions "generally follow
  training requirements from their own institution" [V CITI page]
- [ ] AE/UP reporting lines to all IRBs written into the protocol
