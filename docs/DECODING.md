**What this is:** offline, open-loop decoding of *attempted* 7-DoF arm/hand velocity (cued kinematics recorded during calibration) from binned multi-unit spike counts of one human participant. **What this is not:** closed-loop BCI control. Offline R² does not establish that a user could control a device in real time.

Metric: variance-weighted R² over `eval_mask` samples (FALCON metric). Model: causal exponential filter (τ 240 ms) → z-score (train stats) → ridge (alpha by blocked 5-fold CV inside training data). The first 12 bins of each scored segment are excluded (filter warm-up).

**How to read the controls.** The *mean predictor* (always predict the training-set mean) is the meaningful floor: R² above it means the neural data carries usable information about held-out kinematics. The *time-shift null* (same model, neural data circularly shifted against behavior, 20 offsets) is a weak check: shifted predictions are misaligned but still vary, so they score far below zero, and beating them is easy (even a block that loses to the mean predictor beats them). It is reported for completeness, not as evidence of decoding.
